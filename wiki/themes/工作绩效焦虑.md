The 2026-06-15 diary adds a concrete response: clarify next quarter's direction, add instrumentation, and use data to validate whether optimization points are worth doing. The 2026-06-16 diary sharpens the response: if visible收益, promotion, and salary growth are very hard in the current role, treat the stage as transitional and convert it into transferable capability, including expression ability, planning ability, engineering problem-solving experience, and interview stories. Inference: the key tension appears to be effort-to-recognition conversion under a broader life-direction and career-mobility concern, but the exact performance criteria, validated work plan, and job-change timeline still need evidence.
