# 内在驱动源

## Executive Summary

This is an emerging reflection from the 2026-06-15 diary. The user interpreted 王阳明心学 through the idea that life direction and action are driven by different `<驱动源>`, some arising from the inner self and some shaped by external pressure. A same-day diary update adds that even when the user may have to do some things, they do not want to forget the person they ultimately want to become, the life they want to live, and happiness as the guiding priority. The reflection is not yet a finished principle; the source explicitly says it still needs practical grounding. Source: [2026-06-15 diary](../../sources/diary/2026-06-15-diary.md).

## Principle / Observation

As of 2026-06-15, the user's working interpretation is:

- "心即理" points toward acting from inner `<驱动源>` rather than from drivers formed by external influence.
- "致良知" points toward discerning which drivers are internal and which are externally shaped, especially because `<驱动源>` can disguise itself.
- "知行合一" points toward acting after identifying a driver, then clarifying, polishing, and correcting that driver through real situations.
- The appended "给明天的自己" note adds a concrete value guardrail: unavoidable obligations should not erase the user's desired self, desired life, or happiness as the guiding priority.

This is user-stated interpretation, not a generic doctrine summary. Source: [2026-06-15 diary](../../sources/diary/2026-06-15-diary.md).
