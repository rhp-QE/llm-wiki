# 内在驱动源

## Executive Summary

This is an emerging reflection from the 2026-06-15 diary. The user interpreted 王阳明心学 through the idea that life direction and action are driven by different `<驱动源>`, some arising from the inner self and some shaped by external pressure. ## Principle / Observation

As of 2026-06-15, the user's working interpretation is:

- "心即理" points toward acting from inner `<驱动源>` rather than from drivers formed by external influence.
- "致良知" points toward discerning which drivers are internal and which are externally shaped, especially because `<驱动源>` can disguise itself.
- "知行合一" points toward acting after identifying a driver, then clarifying, polishing, and correcting that driver through real situations.

This is user-stated interpretation, not a generic doctrine summary. Source: [2026-06-15 diary](../../sources/diary/2026-06-15-diary.md).
